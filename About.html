About SME Connect

We are on a mission to empower small and medium enterprises by connecting them with opportunities across Zambia in our digital marketplace.

Our mission

To democratize local trade by providing small and medium enterprises and youth entrepreneurs with the tools, platforms, and connections they need to succeed. We believe every business, regardless of size, deserves access to a wider market.

Our vision

A Zambia where location doesn't limit business growth, where quality services and goods find their rightful markets, and where small businesses can compete on a national stage with the same advantage as large corporations.
